# Design direction

Reading this as: a full-screen virtual office for a solo freelancer, with a warm architectural workspace language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

- **Palette:** chalk and ink neutrals carry long-form project text; deep pine identifies the workspace; copper is reserved for the main action and focus moments.
- **Typography:** the locally available Segoe UI/Aptos system sans keeps dense project and policy details readable without a remote font request; Georgia headings add the editorial character of a personal office.
- **Layout:** the 3D office is the home screen; wall boards and the tablet open the real work modules.
- **Motif:** room labels and a narrow copper rule echo a physical studio index and recur only at actual section boundaries.
- **Cards:** the opportunity list is a continuous queue; only policy and account states use bounded panels because those need a distinct operational boundary.
- **Motion:** agents walk when their real status changes and use quiet idle gestures in shared rooms. Reduced-motion preferences remove ambient and gait motion.
- **3D:** two occupied floors show the actual team and open existing modules through boards and useful office objects. Decorative activity never claims that a job is running.

## Interaction contract

Every navigation item opens a real route. Every action reports its result in the page and is keyboard reachable. Marketplace actions remain governed by the live policy and server safeguards.
